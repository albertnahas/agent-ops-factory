Three kinds of line join the loops. Read by: who reads a loop's output after it runs and catches what it missed. Steered by: the supervisor that writes a loop's Orders, the file that says what it works on now (its tests, priorities and read dates). Escalates to: where a loop sends what needs a person, climbing to the chief of staff, the only one that messages you. Point at a loop to trace its chain; tap it for everything about it.
